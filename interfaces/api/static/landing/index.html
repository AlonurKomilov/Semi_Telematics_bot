Built for every role on your team

Six purpose-built roles — owner, admin, fleet, safety, dispatcher, driver — each with the views and permissions that match the work they actually do.

OWNER

Account Owner

Top-level admin: billing, company setup, integrations (Samsara, Drive), permission overrides, and account-wide reports.

ADMIN

Administrator

Day-to-day management: invite users, assign roles, configure alerts and DND windows, audit log review — without account-level billing access.

FLEET

Fleet Manager

Vehicle telematics, fuel monitoring, maintenance schedules, work orders, fault tracking — everything that keeps the fleet moving.

SAFETY

Safety Officer

Driver Qualification File tracking (49 CFR Part 391), dashcam review, incident scoring, coaching workflow and assignments.

DISPATCHER

Dispatcher

Real-time vehicle position, idle alerts, load assignment, parking-event maps integrated with major telematics providers.

DRIVER

Driver Self-Service

Drivers see their assigned trucks, document expirations, personal scorecards, and shift-end checklists from the Telegram mini-app.

What's inside the platform

Modules every role can use — not separate roles themselves, but the features the platform is built around.

Work Orders & Maintenance

Photo-driven shop invoices from the Telegram bot, structured per-truck folders, payment + parts tracking, expiring-service reminders.

Multi-Company Accounts

One account can hold several carriers — isolated data per company, separate Samsara API keys, per-company scorecards and reports.

Driver Documents

CDL, medical card, MVR, drug screen and DQF documents tracked per driver with expiration alerts and FMCSA-friendly archive on company change.

Scoring & Coaching

Per-driver safety / efficiency / compliance scorecards with coaching workflow assignments — closes the loop on safety events.

Telematics Integration

Real-time telemetry pulled from Samsara per company — fault codes, fuel level, position, idle and parking events, dashcam screenshots.

Telegram Bot & Mini App

Field-friendly bot for drivers and admins: invoice photos, parking checks, alert acknowledgements, plus a Mini App for full dashboard access.

Your documents, your Drive.

Connect your own Google Drive once. Driver licenses, medical cards, truck registrations, and work-order invoices land in your Drive — not our servers.

  • BYOS (Bring Your Own Storage). Files live in your Google account, organized by company.
  • Minimum scope — drive.file. 4truck only accesses files it creates or you explicitly select. Your other Drive files remain private.
  • FMCSA-friendly structure. Per-company folders for driver records, work orders, camera images, and parking maps.
  • Disconnect anytime. Settings → Disconnect Drive. Files stay with you.
