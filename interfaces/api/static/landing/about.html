About 4truck

4truck is vehicle management and operations software for trucking carriers. One platform for Fleet, Dispatch, Safety, HR, Accounting, Recruiter and Driver teams, built on top of the telematics carriers already run.

The company

4truck is built and operated by ABC LEGACY LLC, a software company registered in Wyoming, USA, founded in 2025.

About ABC LEGACY LLC

Founder
