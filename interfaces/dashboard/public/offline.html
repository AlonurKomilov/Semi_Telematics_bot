    Turn Wi-Fi off on your phone and open 4truck. If it loads, your network or internet provider is blocking the connection, not us.

    Ctrl+Shift+N in Chrome, or Ctrl+Shift+P in Firefox. If it loads there, a browser extension is interfering.

    It runs outside our servers, so it answers even when this page can’t — it will say whether 4truck itself is down.
