This network can’t reach 4truck

Your device is online. Something between it and 4truck is refusing the connection — most often an office firewall, a company network policy, or an internet provider.

Your data is safe. Nothing was lost, and nothing was sent anywhere. This page is stored on your device, which is why it still opens.

Get back in now

Any one of these gets you working again while the block is still in place.

4truck reopens by itself the moment a connection returns. If you need it sooner:

  1. Use your phone’s data

    Turn Wi-Fi off on your phone and open 4truck there, or share its connection to this computer. If it loads, the network you were on is what is blocking it.

  2. Connect through a VPN

    A company VPN, or any VPN app. It routes around whatever is blocking the connection, and usually restores 4truck immediately.

  3. If a VPN is already on, turn it off

    Some VPNs break the route instead of fixing it. Worth one try in each direction.

  4. Open a private window

    Ctrl+Shift+N in Chrome, Ctrl+Shift+P in Firefox. If it loads there, a browser extension is interfering.

Get it fixed for good

If you are on an office or company network, whoever runs it can unblock 4truck in a minute. Send them this.

Or check whether it’s us

The status page runs outside our servers, so it answers even when this page can’t. It will say plainly whether 4truck itself is down.
